## Guy's install: 4 months

After upgrading our main panel and roof shingles, it took **121 days** from our call to Tesla until Alameda Municipal Power (AMP) approved our solar panels.

| Day | Date&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; | Step |
|:---:|:----------------------------------:|------|
| 1   | Sep  1 | Went to [TeslaGoSolar.com/contact](https://TeslaGoSolar.com/contact) for a free quote. Tesla added documents to sign, made a solar design from satellite photos, and started filing permits. I accepted payment terms. |
| 27  | Sep 27 | Received email: Site Visit scheduled for Oct 9 _(we called daily until we got an earlier one)_ |
| 39  | Oct  2 | Site Visit took photos of our roof and garage and offered suggestions. |
| 48  | Oct 11 | Tesla emailed: they are working to obtain permits needed for installation. |
| 58  | Oct 21 | Tesla emailed that they received the permits and that installation will start Dec 2. |
| 77  | Nov  9 | Tesla Contractor installed our Powerwall. (driveway needs to be clear) |
| 78  | Nov 10 | Tesla Contractor installed our solar panels and connected the system. <br/> No power or WiFi for 1 hour, then they showed us the basics on the app. |
| 79  | Nov 11 | Tesla Contractor finished wiring (solar panels working)--but didn't install an E-Stop. |
| 80  | Nov 12 | Tesla submitted "Approval to Power On" paperwork. |
| 92  | Nov 24 | Tesla emailed me that an inspector would call me. |
| 96  | Nov 28 | Tesla inspector called me to set up an appointment to inspect on Dec 9. |
| 107 | Dec  9 | Tesla inspector came and said we need an E-Stop (Emergency Stop button) installed first. |
| 113 | Dec 15 | Tesla installed E-Stop; AMP inspected & signed our panel system. (10 min) <br/> Contractor called City Hall Permit Dept; final inspection 12/20 9-12:00. |
| 118 | Dec 20 | Alameda City inspected (10 min) & signed inspection that AMP signed. <br/> I emailed photos of that to AMP for them to process (3-5 business days). |
| 119 | Dec 21 |Tesla requested **$2,000 Down Payment**; I paid; Tesla will notify when processed. |
| 121 | Dec 23 | <mark>AMP approved connection to the grid and Activated (Powered On; Permission-to-Operate)</mark> |
| 187 | Feb 27 | First monthly payment to Tesla. |

**Note:**

- After purchasing, we just waited 3 weeks for a response; but calling would have shorted that wait.
- After the Site Visit and Installation dates were set, we called daily for earlier days and got them.
- Request your Solar Plans. We wasted 1 week because we didn't know they were waiting for us.
- Make sure you have an Emergency E-Stop button installed before inspection to avoid wasting time.
- If an inspector is late, call your City Permit Dept. Our inspection was canceled by an unknown contractor who mistakenly gave our project number instead of their client’s. Inspectors are very organized and prompt.
- Alameda Power will automatically put you in ERG rates. For details, see [Rider-ERG-Effective-2022-7-1](https://www.alamedamp.com/DocumentCenter/View/1069/Rider-ERG-Effective-2022-7-1).
