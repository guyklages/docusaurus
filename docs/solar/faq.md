# FAQ

## Financial

<details>
    <summary> How much do solar panels cost? </summary>

For a typical system:

- 7 - 9 kW
- 1 - 2 batteries

Costs range:

- $19,000 - $28,000
- Financing is about $2,000 down and $180/mo - $240/mo for 10 years

</details>

<details>
    <summary> Is it better to buy or lease solar panels? </summary>

Leasing:

- You _don't_ own them.
- They _won't_ add property value.
_ They _don't_ qualify for any rebates.

Buying:

- Is a better long-term investment.
- And buying a battery is better [for many reasons](./what-we-learned.md#why-2-batteries-are-needed).

</details>

<details>
    <summary> Are there tax rebates for buying solar panels? </summary>

Yes, each state offers different incentives.

**Note:** Leasing doesn't qualify for any rebates.
</details>

<details>
    <summary> How much do solar panels increase home value? </summary>

With panels, **[homes sell 20% faster for 17% more](https://www.solarmelon.com/articles/will-solar-panels-impact-property-value/)**.
</details>

<details>
    <summary> How long until solar panels pay for themselves? </summary>

6 - 9 years, depending on how many batteries you have. {/* TODO: add link to ROI section with graph.*/}
</details>

<details>

As of June 2026, **[32 U.S. states have some sort of solar-panel-related property tax exemption](https://www.paradisesolarenergy.com/blog/does-solar-increase-your-property-taxes/)**.

## Panels

<details>
    <summary> How many panels do I need? </summary>

About 16 - 24 panels (7 - 12 kW), **[depending on your usage and goals](./what-we-learned.md#know-your-solar-goals)**.
</details>

<details>
    <summary> Where are the Tesla panels made? </summary>

USA.
</details>

<details>
    <summary> How long do solar panels last? Will production change over time? </summary>

- Production varies according to the season.
- Solar panel systems degrade in efficiency by ~0.5% / year.
- Systems are guaranteed to be 90% efficient at year 20.
- Your system will last 50+ years.

</details>

<details>
    <summary> How often should panels be cleaned? How to clean them?</summary>

6 - 12 months, only if it doesn't rain within 6 months:

- Rinse off the panels with a water hose from ground level.
- Rinse only the glass cover, not the wiring or underneath area.
- Rinse panels only when overcast or the sun is low.

</details>

<details>
    <summary> What to do about snow or ice on solar panels? </summary>

- Never shovel or use a hard-edged tool.
- The special tempered glass is very good at shedding snow on its own.

</details>

## Batteries

<details>
    <summary> Should I get a battery for my solar panels? </summary>

Yes, absolutely, **[as many as you can afford](./what-we-learned.md#2nd-battery-avoids-grid)**.

**Note:** Without a battery, solar panels don't operate during a power outage for safety reasons.
</details>


## Roof

<details>
    <summary> Is my roof ready for solar panels? </summary>

- If it's old or damaged, you'll need to get it repaired.
- If it's older than 10 years, you'll need to replace it because panels are guaranteed for 20 years.

</details>

<details>
    <summary> Which types of roofs can support solar panels? </summary>

Solar panels can be installed on almost any roof type, except _wood_ or _slate_.
</details>

<details>
    <summary> Which roof direction should solar panels face? </summary>

- South is best for morning and afternoon sun.
- West is better than East if you have morning fog.
- For details, enter your address in **[Google Project Sunroof](https://sunroof.withgoogle.com/)**.

</details>
