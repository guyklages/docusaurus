## Financial

<details>
    <summary> How much do solar panels cost? </summary>

For a typical system:

- 7 - 9 kW
- 1 - 2 batteries

Costs range:

- $19,000 - $28,000
- Finance is $1,000 - $2,000 down and $180-240/mo for 10 years

</details>
